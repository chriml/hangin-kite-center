## Hangin public website: no AI slop

These rules cover visible copy, metadata, alt text, labels, structured data, and public UI. Use the installed `no-ai-slop` skill whenever writing or reviewing public-facing copy. Use `frontend-design` for new or changed public UI. If a skill is unavailable, these project rules still apply.

### Copy

- Write like an experienced Hangin team member at Bulabog Beach: relaxed, direct, useful, and specific.
- Preserve confirmed facts and the writer's natural voice. Never invent prices, availability, ratings, awards, guarantees, instructor names, room details, or wind promises.
- Put the visitor's answer first. Prefer concrete Boracay, beach, wind, lesson, and gear details over adjectives. If a sentence could describe any kite school or tropical island, cut it or make it specific to Hangin.
- Make the minimum useful edit. Leave strong human lines alone; do not polish every paragraph into the same rhythm or structure.
- Cut throat-clearing, faux insight, importance claims, vague attribution, recap endings, rhetorical questions, dramatic fragments, fake-profound kickers, and `not just X, but Y` contrasts.
- Do not use `delve`, `foster`, `leverage`, `utilize`, `facilitate`, `empower`, `streamline`, `robust`, `cutting-edge`, `game changer`, `tapestry`, `realm`, `beacon`, `multifaceted`, `meticulous`, `intricate`, `paramount`, `transformative`, `elevate`, `embark`, `supercharge`, `ever-evolving`, `vibrant`, `seamless`, `showcase`, `enhance`, `pivotal`, or `testament` in public copy. `Harness` is valid as the name of kitesurfing equipment; do not use it as an empty marketing verb.
- Do not use fake urgency such as `book now`, `limited spots`, or countdown language. Calls to action should say what happens next: view lessons, ask about dates, or message Hangin.
- Use no em dashes in short website copy. Avoid decorative colons, emoji headings, scattered bold emphasis, and lists that would read better as a sentence. Real visitor questions and FAQ headings are fine; rhetorical hooks that answer themselves are not.
- Keep SEO language readable. Use search terms where they answer the page's topic; never repeat a keyword for ranking alone.

### Design

- The visual language is Boracay kite culture, not a generic resort, travel blog, or SaaS template.
- Avoid card grids by default, excessive pills and rounded boxes, glassmorphism, purple gradients, floating blobs, tropical-leaf wallpaper, generic icon rows, and decorative motion with no job.
- Use the approved ocean, lagoon, sand, palm, and coral palette; Barlow Condensed and Manrope; real Boracay proof photography; and the kite-line motif with restraint.
- Generated art is supporting illustration only. Never present it as proof of Hangin staff, rooms, facilities, equipment stock, or an actual safari.

### Required review

Before declaring public copy or UI complete, read the rendered page end to end, apply the `no-ai-slop` portability and pattern checks, and run the repository content-style and visual checks. Fix named problems; do not use an AI-detector score or claim that a detector can identify authorship.
