## Proposed operational workflow before lessons, rentals or safaris

### At the first inquiry

Ask only for dates, service, riding level and equipment sizes where relevant. Public notices should ask visitors to obtain participation/intake instructions before sharing sensitive information. Do not ask for diagnoses, medication, medical details or even a yes/no health-condition disclosure in the initial WhatsApp or email exchange. A yes/no answer may itself reveal health data. The approved intake must first define necessity, section 13 basis, notice, recipient access and retention. If the person is under 18, move the conversation to the parent or lawful guardian and give both the adult notice and the age-appropriate child notice before collecting the child's data.

### Before agreement or payment

Give the participant the exact service description, price, inclusions, cancellation and weather terms, and the risks material to that service before taking payment. Those business terms are not in this repository and must be supplied by the owner. Keep the following records together: the risk-notice version, participant agreement version, date and time shown, signer identity and authority, explicit choices, signature evidence and any later withdrawal.

The risk notice and liability agreement must be separate from privacy consent. Neither should be buried in general terms. The participant must have time to read and ask questions. A translated or orally explained version is needed when the participant cannot understand the written language. A minor gets a direct, age-appropriate explanation and a chance to say they do not understand or do not want to take part. Record that assent, but do not treat it as the contract or waiver.

### Before the activity starts

Use a day-specific check rather than relying on the signed paper. Confirm the participant, rider level, conditions, assigned equipment, instructor or guide, and the applicable briefing. Give the participant a real opportunity to stop. Restrict sensitive health details to staff who need them for the declared safety purpose.

The operational checklist needs service-specific content approved by the responsible instructor, insurer and lawyer:

- Lessons: launch and landing area, kite power and lines, releases, falls, collisions, shallow-water impacts, wind and weather changes, bystanders, instructor communication, stop signal and emergency response.
- Rentals: independent-rider threshold, safety-system competence, equipment identity and size, pre-handover inspection, known condition, permitted area and conditions, damage reporting, stop-use rule and return inspection.
- Safaris: route and alternate, unfamiliar launch or landing, distance from shore or the starting point, wind and water changes, communications, regroup and stop rules, inability to continue, rescue and evacuation. Add transport or boat risks only when they are actually part of the trip.

### After the activity

Keep incident records, equipment inspection records and signed documents under approved access and retention rules. Do not reuse health or child data for marketing. Document when records are corrected, access is granted, consent is withdrawn or data is securely destroyed.

For a breach that meets the mandatory-notification threshold and affects children, notify both each affected child and their parent or guardian. Use language the child can understand, alongside the adult notice, and record the recipients, timing and delivery evidence. The DPO must assess the threshold and follow the NPC deadlines and reporting procedure; a guardian-only notice is insufficient. See [NPC Advisory 2024-03, section 4](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Advisory-2024.12.17-Guidelines-on-Child-Oriented-Transparency-w-SGD.pdf), checked 4 September 2026. Notification below the mandatory threshold may also be considered; the advisory does not prohibit it.

## What the waiver cannot safely claim

- It cannot waive future fraud.
- It cannot waive data privacy rights.
- It cannot erase the Consumer Act duties concerning defective services, adequate hazard information or statutory redress.
- It cannot make a minor's signature effective contractual consent.
- It cannot turn a general statement such as “kitesurfing is dangerous” into proof that the rider knew, understood and voluntarily accepted the specific risk that caused an injury.
- It should not promise release from every form of negligence. Philippine law leaves negligence liability and the public-policy limit to the courts, and the Supreme Court has rejected assumption of risk where the organizer failed to control a foreseeable danger.

## Recommended risk controls

These controls support due diligence but do not replace legal review: current staff qualification files; role and supervision records; equipment identification, inspection and maintenance logs; manufacturer recall checks; written condition and no-go criteria; an emergency, rescue and communications plan; drills; incident and near-miss reporting; privacy training; least-privilege access to health and child data; and periodic joint review with the insurer.
