## Executive finding

The code is a static brochure site. The original snapshot exported nine public routes, contains no first-party form, analytics tag, advertising pixel, booking flow, payment flow, cookie-setting application code, browser storage, or first-party data-submission endpoint, and sends visitors to WhatsApp or their email client. That narrow design reduces current collection, but it does not remove Philippine privacy, consumer, tax, business, intellectual-property, tourism, or safety duties.

The repository does not establish the operator's legal name, physical business address, DTI/SEC/CDA record, BIR registration or Registration Seal, DPO, NPC registration status, Cloudflare configuration, DOT accreditation, local permits, instructor credentials, insurance, waiver process, or the operating relationship behind accommodation and safaris. Those are blockers, not blanks that a developer may fill by assumption.

| Finding | Classification | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal operator identity, physical business address, current registrations, and BIR Registration Seal are absent. | Required now | Owner and Philippine counsel must supply and verify the records before the site is treated as launch-ready. |
| Server/CDN request data and inbound WhatsApp or email conversations can be personal data even without a form. | Required now | Establish the PIC, DPO/contact, notice, lawful-basis map, security, retention, rights, vendor, and incident records. |
| The official Trustmark materials conflict about whether registration is mandatory or voluntary. | Required now | Obtain written DTI E-Commerce Bureau guidance and counsel advice. Do not display a badge unless DTI issued it. |
| Prices, sales terms, complaint handling, invoices, discounts, and fulfillment are not implemented on-site. | Launch gate | Do not add online quotation, booking, payment, or checkout until the commerce gates below are closed. |
| The original snapshot had no source-controlled Cloudflare configuration or resolvable DNS from its audit environment; integration adds local `_headers`, with deployed state still unverified. | Required now | Deployment owner must provide dashboard exports and contracts. Repository absence cannot prove a Cloudflare feature is disabled. |
| No clear official source located a blanket WCAG mandate for an ordinary private commercial website. | Recommended control | Use WCAG 2.2 AA as the engineering baseline while counsel checks any sector-specific duty; separately verify physical accessibility duties. |

### Status meanings

- **Required now:** applies to the current business/site or is a current verification blocker. A conditional row remains required to investigate before relying on the condition.
- **Launch gate:** must be completed before the named future feature or practice goes live.
- **Recommended control:** not identified here as a clear blanket legal requirement for this private site, but materially reduces access, privacy, safety, evidence, or consumer risk.

## Trustmark conflict requiring written resolution

The controlling statute, RA 11967 sec. 11, says DTI “shall encourage” development of the Trustmark and allows an industry-led process. The May 24, 2024 IRR sec. 22 retains that approach. DTI's September 15, 2025 notice says DAO 25-12 mandated registration. The DTI Trustmark FAQ, as checked September 4, 2026, contains both of these statements:

- all online merchants, e-marketplaces, e-retailers, and digital platforms **must be Trustmark-registered** to conduct e-commerce; and
- **Application for the Trustmark is voluntary**.

This audit does not choose one sentence over another or assume an extension, exemption, or enforcement position. The blocker closes only when the owner retains current written DTI ECB guidance tied to Hangin's exact transaction flow and a Philippine lawyer records the legal conclusion. Until then, do not represent Hangin as exempt, registered, certified, or permitted to transact by a Trustmark. Do not copy or fabricate the badge.
