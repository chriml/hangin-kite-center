## Source text transcription

Line wrapping has been normalized; wording, capitalization and grammatical errors are retained here so editors can distinguish the source from the public adaptation. See the image for the original layout and prices.

### Introductory Course

> Our Introduction Course is perfect to get an impression of Kitesurfing.
> During this course you will learn the basics of kite control and the safety system.
> The target of this course is to control the kite in safe way.

### Basic Kite Course

> The Basic Kite Course is for all the people who really want to learn Kitesurfing.
> In this course you will learn:
> Set up a kite, use the Safety System, Kite Control, Kite Relaunch, and the first Body Drags downwind.

### Kite Control Course

> The Kite Control Course is the continuation course of the Basic Kite Course.
> In this course you will learn:
> Bodydrag downwind, Bodydrag upwind, Kite Launching and Landing, Self-Rescue, Board Introduction, Board Rescue, First Waterstarts, Board Riding.
> Hopefully you are riding your first meters :)

### Board Riding Course

> The Board Riding Course is the continuation course of the Kite Control Course.
> In this course you will learn:
> Waterstart, Riding Downwind and Riding Upwind.

### Full Course

> From zero to hero!
> Our Full Course makes you an Independent Kitesurfer with full control of the kite and board.

### Advance Course

> In all our Advanced Courses you will have a private instructor who will plan your lesson according to your needs.
>
> Upwind Riding Course / Learn riding upwind like a Pro.
> Freeride Course / Learn different ways to turn and riding toeside.
> Freestyle Course / Learn different ways to jump, Basic Jumps, Transition Jumps, Grabs, Rotations, Unhooking, Loopings and more...
> Surfboard Course/ Learn how to ride strapless with a Surfboard, First Meters, Turns, Jumps and more...
> You will learn to ride independent and safely in all directions.
